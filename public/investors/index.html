Human experience as the engine of growth.

Profitability through an agentic strategy built on how people in the company already think and work.

Thesis

02 / 11

Companies are systems made by people.

Agents improve them only when the human knowledge behind the system is understood.

The usual bet

Capital goes to connecting context and dropping in agents, then hoping the culture follows.

Our bet

Capital goes to reading how work happens, restructuring, then agentizing from that culture.

What the spend misses

The work

05 / 11

Four services. One sequence.

01 Diagnostic Map where expertise lives, how it is used, and which tools and steps are not productive.
02 Change premises Change how the team works and trains: education, roles, and structure. Surface the inefficiencies and the bottlenecks.
03 Core basic agents Implement Tacit and the tools people will actually use, so know-how becomes a living layer.
04 Agentification Ship specific agents that show a visible gain, then extend them with the same people.
